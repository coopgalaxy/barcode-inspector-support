Restore a previous purchase

If Premium is missing after reinstalling the app or moving to another iPhone:

  1. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open the three-dot menu.
  3. Select IMb Premium.
  4. Tap Restore Purchases.

You will not be charged again for restoring the same non-consumable purchase on the purchasing Apple Account.

Purchase troubleshooting

The purchase button is unavailable

Confirm that the device has internet access, in-app purchases are permitted, and the App Store is available. Try again after reopening the app.

Restore Purchases finds nothing

Verify that the iPhone uses the same Apple Account that originally purchased the unlock. If the problem continues, email barcodeinspector@gmail.com.

Payment privacy: Apple processes the purchase. Barcode Inspector does not receive or store your payment-card information.
