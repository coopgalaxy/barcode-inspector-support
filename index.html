The complete handoff

Scan it. Understand it. Send the data where it belongs.

A decoded barcode is only useful when you can do something with it. Barcode Inspector closes the gap between a physical mailpiece and the desktop systems, messages, reports, and people that need its data.

01

Scan on the iPhone

Use the live camera or import an image from Photos. A dedicated scanner handles the distinctive 65-bar USPS IMb.

02

Inspect real fields

Read the tracking code, Barcode ID, Service Type ID, Mailer ID, serial number, routing information, and physical barcode data.

03

Move the result

Copy a single value, copy the full inspection, or send a readable text report through Mail, AirDrop, Messages, or a compatible chat app.
