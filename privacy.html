Barcode Inspector Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Barcode Inspector does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information, scanned barcode values, photographs, or camera images.

Camera and Photos

Camera and photo access are used only to scan barcodes on your device. Images and decoded barcode information are processed locally and are not uploaded to the developer.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment-card information.

Contact

Questions may be sent to barcodeinspector@gmail.com.
